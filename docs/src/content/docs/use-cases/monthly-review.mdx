**Goal:** understand where the money went and fix what went off track.

**Ask:** *“Review September with me.”* — or run the `monthly_review` prompt.

<Steps>

1. **`get_monthly_summary`** gives the month at a glance — income, budgeted, spent,
   Ready to Assign — and only the categories that are overspent:

   <Code code={summary} lang="json" />

2. **`get_category_balances`** and **`get_spending_trends`** explain what stands out:
   is Restaurants high this month, or every month?

   <Code code={balances} lang="json" />

3. Claude proposes fixes — usually moving money from a category with room to the
   overspent one — and applies them with `set_category_budget` once you agree:

   <Code code={budget} lang="json" />

</Steps>

Overspending is fixed by moving money between categories, not by ignoring it: that is
the YNAB method, and the `avenir://guide` resource reminds agents of it. Categories with
nothing budgeted, spent or available are left out unless you ask for `include_empty`.
