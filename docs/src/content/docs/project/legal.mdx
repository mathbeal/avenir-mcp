## What Avenir is meant for

Avenir is meant for **personal use**: one person runs it **on their own machine**, with
**their own YNAB token**, for their own budgets.

<Aside type="caution" title="Not supported: a public or shared server">
Running Avenir as a public or shared MCP server — hosting it for other people, putting
its HTTP transport on the internet, or collecting other people's YNAB tokens — is **not
an intended use** and is not supported:

- YNAB's API terms reserve personal access tokens to their owner; an application used by
  others must use OAuth, with a published data policy. Avenir has neither.
- Avenir has no user accounts, no isolation between users, and its HTTP transport is
  built for this machine only.

Doing so would be at your own risk, and could breach YNAB's terms.
</Aside>

## The EU AI Act

Avenir contains **no AI model**. Its suggestions count which categories a payee received
before; its forecasts use medians and averages. It is a tool that an AI agent calls, not
an AI system of its own. The AI system is your MCP client and its model, whose provider
— Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or another — carries the obligations of Regulation (EU)
2024/1689. Using it for your own budget is a purely personal, non-professional use, and
Avenir is released under a free and open-source licence. It does not assess anyone's
creditworthiness, the credit use the regulation classes as high risk.

This is the publisher's reading, not legal advice. A hosted, commercial version that
embeds a model would call for a new assessment.

## Where your data goes

- Avenir sends your budget data only to **YNAB**, and to the **model provider** your MCP
  client uses (none, with a [local model](/avenir-mcp/guides/choose-a-model/#local-models)).
- The publisher of Avenir receives **nothing**: no telemetry, no account, no server.
- This documentation site sets **no cookies** and runs **no analytics**; its fonts are
  served by the site itself.
