**Goal:** know whether the money lasts, and when it would run out, before it does.

**Tools:** [`forecast_balance`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/forecast-balance/), then
[`set_category_budget`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/set-category-budget/).
**Prompt:** `plan_next_month`.

## The conversation

<Steps>

1. **Ask, with what you know.**

   > **You:** If I earn 3,200 a month, will my accounts go below zero before the end of
   > the year?

2. **The agent projects.** `forecast_balance` with `{"until": "2026-12",
   "monthly_income": 3200}` — two YNAB requests:

   <Code code={projection} lang="json" title="forecast_balance (excerpt)" />

3. **The agent reads the assumptions to you** before the conclusion:

   > **Claude:** Starting from 4,112.66 across Checking and Savings, with 3,200 a month
   > in, your recurring charges (rent 950, groceries about 182.58, electricity 64.20, the
   > train 45, tennis 22, phone 19.99, Streamflix 13.49) and about 69 of other
   > spending, the balance keeps growing: the lowest point, 4,112.66, is today. Anything
   > coming I should add?

4. **You correct.** Add what the history cannot know:

   > **You:** The property tax, 1,426, on 15 October.

   The agent calls again with `one_offs: [{"date": "2026-10-15", "amount": -1426,
   "label": "property tax"}]`.

</Steps>

## What the projection assumes

| Assumption | Where it comes from | Change it with |
|---|---|---|
| Starting balance | today's balance of the open on-budget accounts | `account_ids` |
| Recurring charges and income | the same payee in 3 of the last 4 full months, each month within 20 % of its median | — (returned in `assumptions.recurring`) |
| Other spending | average of the last 3 full months, recurring charges excluded | `variable_monthly` |
| Income | what you give; otherwise the last 3 months' average | `monthly_income` |
| One-off amounts | only what you give | `one_offs` |

Details: [Forecasting](/avenir-mcp/concepts/forecasting/).

## Reading the answer

- `months[].start`, `inflows`, `outflows`, `end`: the month's balance and flows.
- `months[].lowest`: the lowest point within the month, day by day — the one that tells
  you whether a payment would bounce.
- `first_shortfall`: the first month whose lowest point is below zero, or `null`.

<Aside type="caution" title="A projection, not a prediction">
It extends your recent months. A holiday, a yearly tax or a one-off purchase in them
pushes the averages; a new expense is invisible until you add it. Read the assumptions
before the conclusion.
</Aside>

## The charges that come back every month

Ask *"what am I subscribed to, and what does it cost a year?"*: `find_recurring_charges`
lists what the plan pays at about the same amount most months (3 of the last 4), costliest
over a year first, with its category and whether a YNAB schedule already covers it. Regular
spending at one merchant counts too (groceries, here); a charge paid once a year does not,
unless it has a schedule.

<Code code={recurring} lang="json" title="find_recurring_charges" />

## Then budget next month

> **You:** Prepare October's budget from this.

The agent proposes next month's amounts category by category — recurring charges first,
then the rest from recent spending — and applies those you accept with
`set_category_budget`, one previewed change each.
