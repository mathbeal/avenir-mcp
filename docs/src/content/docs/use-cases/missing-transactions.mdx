**Goal:** record what the bank import missed — a pharmacy payment, a cash expense.

**Ask:** *“Add a payment of 32.40 to Pharmacie Centrale on September 21, not imported by
the bank.”*

**`create_transactions`** checks the account, the categories and the dates (not in the
future), then previews what it will add:

<Code code={created} lang="json" />

Once you confirm, the transactions are created **cleared** and, unless you ask for
`approved`, left for you to review in YNAB like any import. `undo_operation` deletes
them.
