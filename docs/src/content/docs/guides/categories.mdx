**Goal:** keep the category structure in line with your life, and give each category its
amount.

**Tools:** [`list_category_groups`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/list-category-groups/),
[`create_category`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/create-category/),
[`update_category`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/update-category/),
[`set_category_budget`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/set-category-budget/),
[`get_underfunded_targets`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-underfunded-targets/).

> **You:** Rename Tennis to Sport.

<Code code={rename} lang="json" title="update_category — preview" />

Pass `category_group_id` to move the category to another group, with or without a new
name. Transactions and amounts stay attached to it. To go back, call again with the
previous name and group, which the answer gives.

## Set a category's amount

> **You:** Put 150 in Restaurants for September.

<Code code={budget} lang="json" title="set_category_budget — preview" />

The amount is **absolute**: it replaces what was budgeted. `month` is `"current"` or
`YYYY-MM-01`; `"current"` is resolved to the actual month so that an undo later acts on
the right one.

## Set a category's target

Ask *"save 1,200 for the holidays by June 2027"* or *"put aside 50 a month for the car"*:
`set_category_target` sets the target YNAB tracks for the category, an amount by a date or
each month, week or year; with no amount it removes the target. Undo restores the previous
target, except one set in YNAB's app as monthly funding, a target balance or a debt payment
when it is replaced or removed; the question says so before you confirm.

<Code code={target} lang="json" title="set_category_target" />

## See which targets are behind

Ask *"which targets are behind this month?"* or *"can I cover my targets?"*:
`get_underfunded_targets` lists the categories whose target still needs money this month,
with what YNAB says each still needs this month (`needed`, its Underfunded amount) and what
the target needs over its whole period (`left`). The most urgent come first: targets due
by a date, the soonest first; then monthly and weekly ones; then the rest, the largest need
first. The totals set what is needed against the month's Ready to Assign, so the agent can
say whether it covers everything, or how much of it.

Here, in September, Transport saves for the rail card renewed on 1 October and Tennis for
the club's membership due on 1 December; Groceries and Restaurants ask for more than is
budgeted. Together they need 155.00, which Ready to Assign covers. The tool changes
nothing: to fund a target, move money to it or set its amount, as above.

<Code code={underfunded} lang="json" title="get_underfunded_targets" />

## Undo

| Tool | Undo |
|---|---|
| `set_category_budget` | `undo_operation` restores the previous amount, unless it changed since |
| `update_category` | call again with the previous name and group |
| `create_category` | hide the category in YNAB — its API cannot delete one |
